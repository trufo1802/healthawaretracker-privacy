# Privacy Policy for HealthAware

Last updated: 2026-04-10

HealthAware ("the App") is a personal health and fitness tracking app for logging calorie, exercise, and sleep information.

## Information We Collect

HealthAware collects information you enter directly in the app, including:

- Calorie and meal logs
- Exercise logs (for example: sets, reps, weight, duration, distance, steps, notes)
- Sleep logs (for example: sleep time, wake time, notes)
- App preferences (for example: theme and tracker settings)

## How We Use Information

Your data is used only to provide app features, including:

- Showing your logs and history
- Generating analytics and charts inside the app
- Saving your personal settings and defaults

## Data Storage

- Data is stored locally on your device using Apple platform storage technologies.
- At this time, HealthAware does not require account creation.
- At this time, HealthAware does not upload your health/fitness logs to a developer-controlled server.

## Data Sharing

- We do not sell your personal data.
- We do not share your personal health/fitness logs with third parties for advertising.
- Data may be disclosed only if required by applicable law.

## Third-Party Services

At this time, HealthAware does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs for tracking user behavior.

## Your Choices

You can:

- Edit or delete data inside the app
- Remove app data by deleting the app from your device (subject to iOS device backup settings you control)

## Children's Privacy

HealthAware is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted at this URL/page with a revised "Last updated" date.
